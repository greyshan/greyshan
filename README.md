# 💫 About Me:
Hey, I'm Gulshan — a self-driven software developer who loves turning ideas into products people can actually use. I enjoy building across the stack, from intuitive web and mobile applications to backend systems and AI-powered solutions. I've worked with startups, independently built a production platform with real users, and experienced what it means to take a product from an idea to deployment. I'm curious by nature, enjoy solving challenging problems, and constantly experiment with new technologies. Outside of building things, you'll probably find me exploring a new field, reading a book, or learning something that challenges the way I think. For me, coding isn't just about writing software; it's about creating, experimenting, and never stopping learning.
